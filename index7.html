Key Data Points to Remember

88% of organizations were hit — but only 54% believe they are a target. The confidence gap is itself a risk.
Only 27% of organizations deploy segmentation — the single highest-leverage control against lateral movement blast radius.
13% of ransom payers recovered all their data. 40% saw it leaked anyway. Paying is not a recovery strategy.
Reputation and brand damage is now the #1 financial cost of ransomware — displacing legal and regulatory actions from 2021.
Only 28% report to law enforcement — forgoing decryption keys, threat intel, and legal pathway support.
