🔑 Key takeaways from Module 1

  • Ransomware is an economic phenomenon — every design choice on both sides is shaped by margin, friction, and risk.
  • The RaaS model separates operators, affiliates, and IABs — disrupting one layer doesn't kill the supply chain.
  • Double extortion (Maze, 2019) made backups alone insufficient — data leaks are a separate crisis.
  • Most effective controls: phishing-resistant MFA, tested immutable backups, EDR everywhere, segmentation.
  • The harder problem is governance, not technology — as you'll explore in Module 6: Tabletop Scenarios.
