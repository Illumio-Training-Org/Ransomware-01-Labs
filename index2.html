🔑 Key takeaways from Module 2

  • Ransomware affiliates spend 3–14 days in your environment before detonating — dwell time is the detection window.
  • LOLBins and legitimate RATs evade signature detection — behavioural context and baselining are the countermeasure.
  • LSASS protection (PPL + Credential Guard) is the single highest-impact credential-access countermeasure.
  • VSS deletion and bcdedit commands are the highest-confidence pre-encryption signals — alert as critical, auto-respond.
  • Rclone renamed to svchost32.exe still has characteristic command-line arguments — detect behaviour, not binary name.
  • If your detection stack only fires on encryption, the attacker has already won.
