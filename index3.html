🔑 Key takeaways from Module 3

  • Backup confidence is the single biggest determinant of bargaining power — if you can recover, the negotiation is only about leak suppression.
  • OFAC strict-liability applies even without knowledge or intent — screen the wallet before every payment.
  • Operators routinely grant 30–70% reductions when victim posture is credible — slow down, document, don't panic.
  • "Paying does not buy silence in 2026" — plan for disclosure regardless of payment decision.
  • The 9 pre-incident questions should be settled now, not during an active incident.
  • Professional negotiators provide threat intelligence, tempo, compliance, and insulation — not just chat typing.
