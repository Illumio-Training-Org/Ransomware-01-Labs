🔑 Key Takeaways — Module 5

  • The SEC clock starts at materiality determination, not detection — document the process rigorously
  • OFAC strict liability means SDN screening is non-negotiable before any ransom payment
  • Legal counsel must retain the IR firm — not the CISO — to preserve work-product privilege
  • CIRCIA, GDPR, NYDFS, HIPAA, and SEC can all run simultaneously on the same incident
  • "Paying quietly" is a myth — ransom payments trigger their own mandatory reporting obligations
