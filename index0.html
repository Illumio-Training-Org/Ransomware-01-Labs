A note on the labs: Each lab is self-contained and runs entirely in your browser — no login, no data stored, no network calls. You can retake any lab as many times as you like. The labs are designed to be completed after reading the corresponding module, but the content cards at the start of each section give you enough context to attempt them cold if needed. The discussion prompts are designed for facilitated debrief but work just as well for solo reflection — hit "Reveal expert answer" when you're ready to compare notes.
