Key Takeaways

Illumio is a breach containment platform: it assumes prevention fails and bounds the blast radius.
Its coverage is strongest in the middle of the lifecycle — discovery, lateral movement, and containment.
Only 27% of organisations deploy segmentation — Illumio is, by name, the control that closes that gap.
It reinforces, never replaces, the identity, endpoint, recovery, and legal disciplines around it.
